Offline

Works without signal

Tasks, habits, tags and history live in a local database on the device. Nothing is uploaded to hold your list, so the app is fast on a plane and never waits on a spinner.

Transcripts

Voice notes that stay readable

Every take is transcribed on the device while you speak, so a rant is searchable text minutes later instead of an audio file you have to replay.

Focus

A built-in timer

A 25/5 pomodoro sits one tap away for when the list is done and the work is not.
