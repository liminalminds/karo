Everything in one list

A task manager that listens

Tasks are the obvious part. Karo is built around the parts that usually get abandoned a week in: the habits, the streak, the tags and the follow-through.

🎤

Reads your rants

Hold the button and talk. The transcript is written while you speak, and the mess of it is split into real items — no typing, no forgetting what you meant to add while you were saying it.

⚙️

Automates the admin

Date words become deadlines, “every day” becomes a habit, and tags you mention get filed on the spot. You review the draft instead of building it.

🎯

Habit tracking

Habits live beside your tasks with their own daily counts. Want water eight times? That is one habit with a target of eight, not eight things to tap.

🔥

Streaks that mean something

A day only counts when everything expected that day was finished. Add a habit today and yesterday’s streak stays exactly where it was.

📅

History & reminders

Every day closes into a frozen record of what you said you would do and whether you did. Missed days surface before they quietly vanish.

🏷️

Goals without the grind

A goal is just a recurring habit you care about. Progress shows up as counts and streaks instead of a project plan you abandon on day four.

Say it the messy way

Hold the phone, rant for forty seconds, stop caring about grammar. Karo captures the audio and writes the transcript at the same time, so nothing is lost between the thought and the list.

Work groups & lists

Separate worlds, one app

Work, home, a renovation, the list of films you owe a friend. Each gets its own checklist, and tags cut across all of them so you can still ask “what is on my plate today” and get one answer.

Tags that colour the whole app

Give a tag a colour once and every task, habit and list item that carries it picks it up. Sorting by tag is a glance, not a query.

#work #health #home #money #reading

Deadlines you cannot miss

Anything with a date shows up on its own day, and the day it slips into the past gets called out.

Repeating work

Daily, weekly, monthly or once. Set it once and it keeps coming back.

Today is a view, not a list to maintain

Open the app and you see what is due today, what is unfinished, and how far along the day is. Everything else is exactly where you left it — there is no inbox to empty.
