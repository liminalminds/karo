Privacy policy

The short version: your lists live on your device, and only a recording you explicitly send for analysis ever leaves it.

💾

What stays on device

Your tasks, habits, tags, reminders, history and voice notes are stored in a database on the device. Karo has no server account to sign into and keeps no copy of them anywhere else.

🎤

What leaves the device

Exactly one thing, on purpose: a recording you explicitly send for analysis. That audio is used to produce the transcript and candidate items, is kept only long enough to answer, and is not used to advertise to you or train a general model on your rants.

🚫

What Karo never does

No accounts, no contact lists, no location tracking, no third-party ad or tracking SDKs, no selling of data. There is nothing about you to sell because we never collected it.

⚙️

The on-device transcript

The transcript is produced on device. What you read in the app while editing a draft never needed a round trip, and once you confirm the items the recording itself can be deleted from inside the app.

⚖️

Analysis is the paid part

The app runs on its own. Analysis of recordings is the only feature that costs Karo anything to serve, which is why it is the only paid feature. Payments are handled by the app store and Karo does not receive or store card details.

📦

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the on-device database. Because there is no server side account, there is no lingering profile to request deletion of — the data goes when the app goes.

Questions about this policy? Reach us from the about page. See also the terms of use.
